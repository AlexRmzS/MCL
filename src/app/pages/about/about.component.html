About Maid Café Latte

Our Story

Founded in 2020, Maid Café Latte brings a unique blend of Japanese maid café culture and high-quality coffee to the heart of Monterrey. Our passion for creating a warm, welcoming atmosphere has made us a beloved destination for both locals and visitors.

What started as a small dream has blossomed into a thriving café that combines exceptional service with delicious food and beverages. We pride ourselves on creating an experience that transports our guests to the charming streets of Akihabara, Tokyo.

Our Mission

At Maid Café Latte, our mission is to spread joy and create memorable experiences through delicious food, exceptional coffee, and unique, attentive service. We strive to be more than just a café – we aim to be a community where everyone feels welcome.

We are committed to sourcing the finest ingredients, supporting local suppliers, and implementing sustainable practices to reduce our environmental footprint while delivering unforgettable culinary experiences.
